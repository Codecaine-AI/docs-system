Variation B — Layered Color

Process Outline block, restyled so color carries the hierarchy. Every nesting level draws its rail, elbow and arrowhead in its own hue from a five-step muted cycle (bracket-pair coloring, kept desaturated); actor chips and note cards inherit their layer's hue; loop keywords take a separate warm accent. Spacing rises only moderately over the baseline because the color is doing the work.

Depth cycle (light / dark): 1 steel 2 sage 3 plum 4 teal 5 ochre keyword accent

Token sheet

Every custom property this variation reads, with its light and dark value. Tier-2 naming follows the existing --docs-process-outline-* family, so nothing here needs a new namespace. Tint strengths ship as percentages rather than pre-mixed colors: the mix has to happen at the use site, where the cascading layer color --po-c is in scope.
